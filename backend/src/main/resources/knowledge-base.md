## OVERVIEW
BookLeaf Publishing is a self-publishing company operating in India and the US. Packages: Standard Free (no upfront cost) and Bestseller Breakthrough (premium, paid, with marketing and distribution add-ons). BookLeaf handles cover design, typesetting, ISBN assignment, printing, distribution and royalty management. The in-house printing facility and warehouse are in Delhi; overflow and special formats go to print partners Repro India and Epitome Books. Books are listed on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK and the BookLeaf Store.

## TONE
Always empathetic and professional: authors are partners, not customers to be managed. Acknowledge the concern before jumping to solutions. Be specific: use actual numbers, dates and statuses instead of vague reassurance. If something is BookLeaf's fault (delayed royalties, ISBN error), own it directly with no corporate deflection. If the issue needs escalation or investigation, give a clear timeline such as "Our team will look into this and get back to you within 48 hours" instead of open-ended promises. Always end with a clear next step for the author and/or the BookLeaf team.

## ROYALTY_PAYMENTS
Royalty split is 80/20: 80% of net profit per book goes to the author, 20% to BookLeaf. Net profit = MRP minus printing cost, platform commission (Amazon/Flipkart) and shipping charges. Royalties are calculated quarterly and paid within 45 days of the quarter ending. Minimum payout threshold is Rs 1,000; below that, royalties roll over to the next quarter. Payouts are made by bank transfer to the account linked in the author's dashboard. Authors can see a detailed royalty breakdown with sales per platform in their dashboard.
Example, no royalty after 4 months: acknowledge the frustration; explain the quarterly cycle and 45-day payout window; ask the author to confirm bank details are linked; give the specific next payout date; if genuinely overdue, escalate with a 48-hour resolution timeline.
Example, amount seems too low (200 copies sold, Rs 3,000 received): explain the net profit calculation (MRP minus printing, platform commission and shipping); offer a detailed line-by-line royalty breakdown; do not be defensive, offer transparency.

## ISBN_METADATA
Every book published through BookLeaf receives a unique ISBN assigned by BookLeaf, registered under BookLeaf's publisher imprint. An author who wants an ISBN under their own imprint must obtain it independently. ISBN errors (duplicate, wrong book linked) are treated as high priority and escalated to the production team.
Example, different ISBN on Amazon than on the physical copy: treat as high priority; acknowledge this is a serious data issue; confirm escalation to the production team immediately; give a 48-hour resolution timeline.

## PRINTING_QUALITY
In-house printing handles most orders; overflow or specific formats go to Repro India or Epitome Books. Standard print turnaround is 5-7 business days from order confirmation. For quality issues (misprints, binding defects, colour inconsistency) BookLeaf arranges a free reprint after verification; the author may need to share photos of the defective copy.
Example, blurry images and misaligned pages: apologise sincerely; ask for photos of the defective copies; confirm a free reprint once verified; give a 5-7 business day reprint timeline.

## DISTRIBUTION
Books are listed on Amazon India, Flipkart, Amazon US, Amazon UK and the BookLeaf Store. New listings typically go live within 7-10 business days after publication is complete. A book showing as unavailable on a platform usually indicates a stock sync issue; BookLeaf can trigger a re-sync within 24-48 hours.
Example, "Currently Unavailable" on Amazon: explain it is typically a stock sync issue; confirm you are triggering a re-sync with the distribution team; set the expectation of 24-48 hours.

## PRODUCTION_STATUS
Stages: Manuscript Received, Editing (if opted), Cover Design, Typesetting, Proofreading, ISBN Assignment, Printing, Distribution Setup, Published and Live. Authors are emailed at each stage. Delays typically happen at Cover Design (waiting for author approval) and Proofreading (revision rounds).
Example, 3 weeks in typesetting: check the actual production status; if delayed, be honest about why (for instance waiting on author approval for proofs); give a specific updated timeline; never blame the author even if the delay is on their side, frame it collaboratively.

## GENERAL_INQUIRY
Metadata updates (such as a book description on Amazon) can be submitted through the dashboard or by emailing the BookLeaf team. Changes typically reflect on platforms within 3-5 business days.
Example, updating the description of a live book: say yes, explain how to submit it, and give the 3-5 business day timeline.
